## About Me

**Architecting order inside distributed chaos.**

I am a Computer Science student and cloud systems builder with a strong focus on **AWS, DevOps, and MLOps**. I currently serve as **Captain of the AWS Cloud Club – PCU Cavite**, where I lead technical initiatives, hands-on workshops, and community-driven projects focused on cloud and AI adoption.

My engineering approach is grounded in embracing complexity early, structuring it deliberately, and operationalizing systems through **automation, observability, and governance**. I am particularly interested in building resilient architectures that scale without sacrificing clarity or control.
